Terra Classic Wallet

Your keys stay on this device, so nobody else can move your funds and nobody else can recover them for you.

Set up your wallet

Pick a phrase length, then a PIN. The keys are encrypted with it and kept in your phone's secure storage, never on a server.

12 words is 128 bits of entropy. Nothing will brute force that, and it is far easier to write down without a mistake.

There is no reset. If you forget this PIN or lose the phone, the recovery phrase on the next screen is the only way back in.

Your recovery phrase

These words ARE your wallet. Write them down in order. Anyone who reads them owns your funds.

Tap to reveal

Make sure nobody is looking at your screen

Telegram cannot block screenshots in a Mini App, and the clipboard is readable by other apps. Paper is safer than both.

I have written the phrase down and stored it somewhere safe.

Check your copy

Drag the two matching words from below into their places. Two of the ten belong to your phrase.

Drag a word, or tap it and then tap a slot. Tap a filled slot to send the word back.

Restore a wallet

Enter your 12 or 24 word recovery phrase, separated by spaces.

Never enter a phrase somebody gave you. A phrase you did not create is a trap, and whatever you send there will be taken.

Watch an address

See balances, staking and history without giving Frontier any keys. Nothing can be sent or signed in this mode, and you can add a key later.

Use this to try Frontier before trusting it with anything. Never paste a recovery phrase into an app you are still deciding about, including this one.

Watching

Frontier holds no keys for this address. Sending and staking stay locked until you import the recovery phrase.
